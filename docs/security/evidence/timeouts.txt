# Slow and idle connections (WSTG-v42-CONF-02)
# Caddy at https://localhost and http://localhost, vaultd at http://127.0.0.1:8090.
# Caddy defaults from https://caddyserver.com/docs/caddyfile/options: read_header 1m, idle 5 minutes.

## Request headers sent one line per second, never finished
Caddy :443 before F19   closed after 60.0 s (default read_header 1m)
Caddy :443 after F19    closed after 10.0 s
Caddy :80 after F19     closed after 60.0 s (the redirect server kept the defaults, F23)
Caddy :80 after F23     closed after 10.0 s
vaultd                  closed after 10.0 s (ReadHeaderTimeout)

## Idle keep-alive connection after one request
Caddy :443 before F19   5 minutes, the documented default (not measured)
Caddy :443 after F19    closed after 120 s
Caddy :80               the redirect answers with Connection: close
vaultd                  2 minutes (IdleTimeout)

## JSON body of 1000 declared bytes sent one byte per second
vaultd                  400 after 30 s (ReadTimeout)
through Caddy           vaultd ends the request after 30 s (its log: duration_ms 30000);
                        the client receives the 400 after 90 s
